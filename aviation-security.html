Aviation Security

We support airport operations with vigilant personnel and reassuring passenger assistance.

Discuss your requirements

Attentive security.
A smoother passenger experience.

Winstar deploys security personnel at airport gates, entrances and scanning stations. We combine vigilance with clear, courteous communication, helping protect passengers and terminal staff while supporting everyday operations.

Gates & entry points

We staff designated access points, check entry permissions and help prevent unauthorised movement, following the operator’s procedures for each post.

Screening-area support

Our personnel support orderly movement around scanning stations, guide passengers and report concerns to the responsible screening team under the agreed assignment.

Passenger & staff assistance

We provide a calm point of contact, explain directions clearly and refer requests to the appropriate airport team, keeping security duties attentive and professional.

Vigilance & incident reporting

Our teams monitor their assigned areas, recognise unusual activity and escalate concerns through agreed channels, maintaining clear records for follow-up.

Prepared personnel.
Coordinated operations.

We work with your airport or aviation team to define each post’s duties, operating hours and reporting lines. Our deployment plan aligns personnel with the location, passenger flow and responsibilities of the assignment.

Assignment preparation

We review duties, site procedures and required training and access permissions before deployment, so personnel understand their role and its boundaries.

Supervision & communication

We support personnel with supervision and clear communication, coordinating handovers and referring operational concerns to the designated airport contacts.

Incident coordination

Our teams follow the site’s escalation procedures, assist within their assigned responsibilities and coordinate with authorised responders when further action is needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you work alongside our existing airport security team?+

Yes. We agree duties, supervision and reporting lines with your team, integrating our personnel into the site’s operating procedures.

Does the service include operating screening equipment?+

Our core offer includes personnel at scanning stations and access points. Equipment operation is a separate duty that depends on the required training, authorisation and agreed scope.

Can staffing follow our operating schedule?+

We plan shifts and handovers around your operating hours and expected activity, confirming staffing levels and responsibilities before deployment.

What information do you need for a proposal?+

Share the facility, areas requiring coverage, operating hours and proposed duties. We then discuss site procedures, personnel requirements and your preferred start date.
