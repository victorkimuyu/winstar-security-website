Biometric & Card-Based Access

We put you in control of who enters, where and when.

Request a quote

Choose how people enter

Cards and fobs

We configure card and fob access for your staff, residents and contractors. Compatible systems let your administrators disable lost cards and remove access when someone leaves.

Biometric readers

We install fingerprint or facial recognition readers suited to your users and entrances. We help you plan enrolment and alternative access for anyone who cannot use the reader.

How entry works

  1. Present a credentialUse a card or an enrolled biometric.
  2. Check permissionsThe system checks the door and any schedule in effect.
  3. Grant or deny entryIf access is approved, the system releases the lock.

Access that fits
the way you work

We tailor access rules to your premises, from shared lobbies to restricted stockrooms. We plan around your priority doors, their users and changing access needs throughout the day.

Set permissions by role

We configure supported door and time permissions for each group, with separate arrangements for visitors and temporary contractors.

Review access events

We configure activity records on compatible systems, with access and retention settings agreed for your authorised staff.

Check what can work together

We assess your doors, locks and readers, then integrate compatible equipment with CCTV or turnstiles and barriers where useful.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we use our existing cards or readers?+

Possibly. We check your card formats, readers and controllers together to identify what we can retain.

What happens during a power cut?+

It depends on the lock, controller and backup power. We agree each door’s outage behaviour with you and test exit arrangements before handover.

What should we consider before using biometrics?+

We help you assess the need for biometrics, plan enrolment data protection, assign administrators and choose an alternative access method.
