ON-SITE PROTECTION

Attentive to people.
Alert to what matters.

Good guarding balances vigilance with a helpful, professional manner. Duties are shaped around the site, its activity and the level of protection required.

01

Entry and movement

Control access for people and property, with attention to the movement of staff, visitors and deliveries.

02

Vigilance and incident handling

03

04

Provide a courteous point of contact for people using your premises, bringing customer care into everyday security duties.

YOUR QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Planning your
guarding service.

A practical starting point for discussing duties, personnel and support.

What duties can a Winstar guard undertake?+

Duties can include managing the movement of people and property, maintaining occurrence books and incident reports, assisting visitors and responding to incidents. The assignment should reflect your premises and the responsibilities agreed for the site.

How are guards selected and prepared?+

Winstar’s selection process includes vetting and interviews. Training covers access control, customer care, public relations, and health, safety and environmental awareness, alongside practical guarding skills.

How are guards supported while on duty?+

The service is supported by supervisory patrol checks and radio communication coordinated through the control room. Occurrence books, incident reports and daily summaries provide a written record of activity.

Can guarding be arranged for day and night coverage?+

Winstar provides round-the-clock guarding arrangements. The staffing level, shift pattern, duties and support arrangements for your premises are agreed as part of the service scope.

What should I share when requesting a guarding quote?+

Tell us the site location, type of premises, operating hours, entrances to be covered and your preferred start date. Explain any visitor or delivery requirements. Detailed security procedures can be discussed directly with the team.
