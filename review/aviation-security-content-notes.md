# Aviation Security — first draft

15 November 2026.

## Evidence and scope

Winstar's profile, PDF page 10, documents trained personnel at gates, scanning stations and entrances, protecting passengers and terminal staff with a calm professional presence. General profile material supports access control, supervision, reporting and incident coordination.

The draft describes those duties in active language. Presence at scanning stations does not establish authorisation to operate screening equipment. The FAQ distinguishes these responsibilities. No KCAA/ICAO certification, aircraft search, baggage/cargo screening, unrestricted airside access or airline contract is claimed.

## Comparative references

- [G4S UK aviation](https://www.g4s.com/en-gb/sectors/aviation-security): connects aviation security to passenger experience and operational flow.
- [Securitas aviation services](https://www.securitas.com/en/security-services/safety-and-security-services/aviation-security/): distinguishes airport and airline services. Its specialist aircraft and screening capabilities are not imported into Winstar's offer.
- [ICAO function-specific competency mapping](https://www.icao.int/sites/default/files/Security/documents/ICAO-AVSEC-Function-Mapping-Table.pdf): role-specific access control, reporting and communication context. Used to keep duties bounded, not to imply certification or provide regulatory advice.
