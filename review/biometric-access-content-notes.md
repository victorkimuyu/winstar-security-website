# Biometric and card-based access

Winstar's company profile, PDF page 11, establishes biometric and card-based access as part of its offer. The page presents credentials, door permissions and practical planning for offices, shared lobbies and restricted rooms. Equipment features and integrations depend on the site and selected system.

- [Hikvision access-control product overview](https://securitylab.hikvision.com/us-en/products/access-control-products/) describes card, fingerprint and facial readers.
- [Hikvision reader authentication documentation](https://enpinfo.hikvision.com/hkwsen/unzip/20230830144955_12918_doc/GUID-2301463B-F470-4C69-86DA-9961B82E3894.html) describes device-dependent authentication methods and schedules.
- [Hikvision access-event documentation](https://www.hik-connect.com/views/terms/newHelp/gv_helpIos/GUID-97C80616-33D0-4B90-B3AA-4AE6D35C1663.html) supports the description of event records on compatible systems.
- [HID card and credential guidance](https://support.hidglobal.com/cards-and-credentials) supports checking card formats against readers before an upgrade.

These sources explain common capabilities; they do not establish a Winstar supplier partnership or guarantee particular models. The Contact Us page uses the existing company contact details and a prefilled email for quote requests.
