## What the supplied brief says

The intended philosophy is proactive protection: deterrence, a reassuring visible presence, vigilant guards, CCTV coverage, smart alarms, connected security components, rapid response and professional incident resolution. The customer outcome is peace of mind for clients and their guests.

The initial brief names five service groups. The corrected profile establishes a broader eight-service catalogue: manned guarding; alarm monitoring and response; dog security; VIP and cargo escorts; aviation security; electronic security; facility management; and electric fencing (PDF pp.6–13). Electronic security includes equipment sourcing/installation, CCTV, toll systems, automated barriers and biometric/card access. Turnstiles and bollards appear in the initial brief, but are not specifically named in the profile.

The five named sectors are real estate, banking, aviation, warehousing, and hotels and recreation. Aviation now has an explicit service description. The profile's 17 client logos supply potential evidence across banking, logistics, hospitality, property, education and other corporate work; current relationships and exact assignments still need confirmation.

## Working understanding of the business

The profile describes Winstar as founded in 1997 and incorporated in 2001, with more than 2,000 personnel, a Mombasa head office and branches in Nairobi, Nakuru, Eldoret and Kisumu (PDF pp.2, 4–5). Its quick-facts panel labels 2001 as both founding and incorporation; dates and dated workforce counts need reconciliation.

The connected-protection story now has direct support: three-week guard training, patrol supervision, radio/control-room coordination, emergency crew dispatch and incident reporting (p.6); alarm monitoring and mobile response (p.7); tracking/control-room-supported cargo escorts (p.9); CCTV/alarm integration (p.11); and fence/alarm integration (p.13). Dedicated account management is described on p.4.

The profile also claims PSRA registration and pioneer KSIA membership (p.2). These are useful trust points to substantiate with current documents. A complementary facility-management offer broadens the business beyond security alone.

Likely website buyers include property managers, facilities teams, procurement managers, security managers and business owners. They need to understand service fit, geographic availability, evidence of reliability and how to obtain a tailored proposal. These audience assumptions should be validated with the client.

## Recommended proposal direction

**Proactive protection. Reassuring presence.**

Use this as a proposed organising message. Support it with a simple customer story: deter threats, detect concerns, control access and coordinate response. Connect every stage to confirmed services and real evidence.

The opportunity is to make Winstar's offer easier to understand and evaluate. Integrated protection and peace of mind are already common competitor themes. Differentiation must come from Winstar's actual delivery practices, relevant experience, people and client relationships—not an unsupported claim of being Kenya's best or fastest.
