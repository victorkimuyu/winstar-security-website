## Winstar evidence and scope

The user explicitly includes Event Security in the agreed offering. Supplied profile and notebook material support guard vetting, customer care, access control, supervision, communication and incident response, but contain no detailed event-specific case study. Common event duties form the draft; final assignment scope is agreed with organisers.

Do not claim named event experience, attendance capacity, fixed guard-to-guest ratios, foreign certifications, drones, armed staffing, medical provision, ticketing-system supply or responsibility for the whole event's safety. Emergency content describes coordination with the venue and designated responders. Parking duties are within the venue, not authority over public roads.

## Page decisions

- A short, customer-focused hero and existing Winstar team photograph, used as personnel imagery rather than evidence of an event deployment.
- Split scope section: entry/screening, queues/movement, restricted areas/equipment and parking. Reuse the muted Alumni Sans numbering.
- One slate operational panel organised by before, during and after the event.
- Four FAQs about staffing, venue coordination, setup/overnight cover and quote details.
- Existing CTA, typography, shared header/footer and responsive layouts; no extra icon tiles or eyebrows.
- Concise active language describing Winstar's work. Event-specific photography and browser visual review remain for refinement.
