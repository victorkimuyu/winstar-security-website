# Face anonymisation — 18 November 2026

Eight current website assets updated with fictional face variants generated using the built-in image tool. Original assets retained. The generated face regions were integrated into original decoded pixels; lossless WebP preserves all pixels outside those regions. An audit with exact source/output paths and bounded regions is saved in anonymisation-audit.json.

Winstar response pickup: only the two guards' face regions are edited. The entire lower vehicle/body region was checked for pixel equality against the original. Paint, logos, number, plates, geometry and equipment retained.

Slide 2 is the explicit exception: visitor car painted blue, female guard given a deeper-complexioned fictional face, glasses removed and braided hairstyle under the same cap; driver's face changed too. No Winstar fleet car was recoloured.

Previously generated wholly illustrative scenes remain as illustrations; this pass targets the eight assets derived from identifiable supplied photographs. Perimeter worker has no clearly visible face.

Outputs visually reviewed together in anonymous-review.jpg. All shared references across the homepage, service pages and overview point to the new assets. Source folders and previous derivatives remain available privately in the project; this task did not remove source photographs.
