# Winstar photography review

18 November 2026. Reviewed every image in both supplied Resized folders: 60 in WINSTAR PHOTOS and 144 in WINSTAR PHOTOS 2 (204 total, including duplicates and near-duplicate sequences). Nine contact sheets cover the collection. Twenty candidates are in the clickable `shortlist.html` gallery, with exact paths, dimensions, placement suggestions and editing notes.

## Recommended direction

Use the authentic photographs to demonstrate people, working methods and operational resources. Choose active work over repeated posed line-ups. Use distinct images for hero and supporting sections, and avoid showing the same personnel/vehicle sequence repeatedly on one page.

Most shortlisted originals are 3000 × 2000, sufficient for useful crops and normal desktop display. A 3:1 hero crop keeps only half the height of a 3:2 photograph, so check heads, hands, equipment and vehicle identities carefully. For action that cannot survive that crop, keep a taller supporting frame or extend only background. Preserve people, uniforms, devices and actual activity during edits.

## Keep current imagery or obtain a specific photograph

- **Biometric/card access:** no clearly identifiable reader-use photograph found. Retain the current illustration until genuine equipment/user photos are available.
- **Turnstiles/bollards/barriers:** gates and vehicle checks are present, but no clear turnstile or bollard installation. Do not relabel a manual gate photo as that equipment.
- **Aviation:** no identifiable airport-screening deployment found in this collection. Keep the current illustrative checkpoint distinct from evidence of an actual deployment.
- **Electric Fencing:** wall-work photographs show coiled razor wire prominently. Use as general perimeter maintenance only unless Winstar identifies visible electric-fence components. Generating electric strands into a real installation photo would misrepresent that site.
- **Event hero:** event-branded photos exist, but are smaller and mostly rear/side views. The current generated guest-check scene remains more suitable for the wide hero composition; use genuine event photos to support it.
- **Named sector/client claims:** venue logos and site names are not sufficient to establish a current contract. Do not infer airport, banking or logistics contracts from generic uniforms/vehicles.

## Editing priorities

1. Prepare honest wide crops for Security Guards, CCTV, fleet and control-room candidates.
2. Compare crops at desktop and mobile proportions before generating anything.
3. Where essential action would be lost, extend background in a separate asset, preserving people and operational detail.
4. Lightly correct exposure/colour where needed; keep skin tones, uniform colours and real logos consistent.
5. Retain originals and record provenance for every derivative.
