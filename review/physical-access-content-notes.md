# Turnstiles, bollards and barriers

Built 9 November 2026 on the existing Access Control route. Winstar's notebook (PDF page 4) explicitly lists turnstiles, barriers and bollards; its profile describes automated barriers and linked electronic access.

The page separates pedestrian entry, vehicle restriction and controlled vehicle lanes. It covers site assessment, compatible readers, movement planning, installation, handover, maintenance and four FAQs. No manufacturer partnership, impact rating, throughput guarantee or particular stock model is claimed.

Manufacturer references:

- [CAME automatic barriers](https://www.came.com/global/en/products/automatic-barriers/): vehicle-lane controls, supporting detection devices and equipment-specific backup options.
- [Hörmann bollards](https://www.hormann.co.uk/home-owners-and-renovators/bollards/): fixed, removable and retractable configurations.
- [CAME high-security bollards](https://www.came.com/global/en/products/high-security-bollards-one-50-evo-retractable/): illustrates that impact resistance is a specific tested product characteristic, not a promise made for every bollard.

## Design revision — 9 November 2026

Follow the older Security Guards, CCTV and K9 pages: a light photographic introduction, open two-column service scope, slate delivery panel, pale FAQ section and closing quote CTA. Reuse shared styles rather than extending the biometric page’s separate hero treatment.

The existing profile photograph of a guard at an entrance gives operational context; it is not presented as an equipment installation. Product photography remains a later refinement opportunity.

Replace repeated three-column bands with an introduction beside three equipment descriptions. Each description explains what the equipment controls and where it is useful. Combine integration, installation and ongoing support into one delivery panel, removing the redundant support section. Retain established typography, red accent, FAQ and CTA treatments.
