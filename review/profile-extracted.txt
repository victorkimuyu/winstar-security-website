1. Manned Guarding
Winstar Security Guards offers manned professional guarding services to different type of
clients by determining the appropriate level of protection and understanding each client’s
situation as unique and can change with time.
Our guards undergo rigorous vetting and interview conducted by our senior operation
management team thereafter going through our three week training program. Areas covered
in our training include drill, access control, public relation, customer care and health, safety and
environmental awareness making our guards professional, responsive and reliable in service
delivery.
Manned guarding service is done on 24 Hours basis through random patrol checks and
radio communication coordinated by our control room. We offer promo response during
emergencies by dispatching patrol vehicle with crew members. We also ensure proper record
keeping through writing of occurrence book, incident reports and daily summary.
Guards’ responsibilities include;
1. Access control of personnel and property
2. P reparing occurrence book, daily summary and incident reports
3. Ensuring safety of personnel and property
4. Provision of necessary assistance to the public
5. Responding and Handling of emergencies

2. Alarms Monitoring and Response
We offer residential and commercial alarms that secure your home or business premises with
simple radio monitoring alarms or intruder detection systems. Our nationwide VHF and GSM
communication networks allow us to dispatch Mobile Response Teams immediately.
Manual Alarm System
This is a basic push and alert system that is activated manually by pressing a fixed or wireless
remote button. The alarm activation will be received in our Control Room and a mobile response
team will be dispatched to your home or business immediately.
Automatic Alarm System
This is the most preferred system and far superior to the manual alarm system. The system
allows 24 hour coverage irrespective of whether the client is present at home or the office.
Once a sensor is triggered, the signal is received at our Control Room and a mobile response
team is dispatched immediately. The system is triggered from a variety of sensors such as door
magnetic contacts, vibration sensors or infra-red motion sensor.

3. Dog Security
Our K9 dog section is composed of different dog breeds ranging from German Shepherds,
Rottweiler and Boer bells each specifically trained and provided with proper medical care and
nutrition. By fully understanding your security requirements and associated risks; we will provide
you with a bespoke service solution.
Patrol Dogs
The dogs are assigned a dog handler that is trained in dog care, obedience and attack tactics.
The dogs are able to deter hidden intruders or items by sight, scent and sound. Patrol dogs are
a proven asset in theft reduction and can be effective due to their excellent olfactory system.
We have a punctual delivery system for residential and commercial properties with our purpose
built deployment vehicles.
Sniffer Dogs
The dogs are trained to deter and indicate presence of explosive or narcotic material. They are
ideal for explosive detection at airports, stadium and shopping malls.

8. Electric Fencing
We install, repair and service all types of electric fences to deter intruders from breaking into
your residential and commercial property. The electric fence works by sending a high voltage to
the tune of 2,500- 12,000 volts pulse whenever an intruder touches the live fence.
Our fences have the capability to act as alarm triggers once connected to our transmitters. Any
activation alerts our mobile response teams to visit the site and investigate the cause of alarm. A
survey must be conducted to determine the type of electric fence that suits the client.
