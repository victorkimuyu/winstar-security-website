# VIP & Cargo Escorts — first draft

15 November 2026.

## Evidence

The supplied Winstar company profile, PDF page 9, explicitly describes:

- VIP close protection and advance venue reconnaissance.
- Personnel trained in VIP protection, de-escalation, emergency and first-aid procedures.
- Sensitive cargo escorts from origin to destination.
- Tracking of escort vehicles/personnel and real-time monitoring through a 24-hour control room.

The draft distinguishes these two offers. Monitoring language belongs to cargo escorts, as in the source. It does not turn training in first aid into a medical service claim. General assignment planning, communication and arrival coordination provide context without adding specialist capabilities.

## Comparator references

- [BM Security specialised services](https://bmsecurity.com/solutions/specialised-services/): tailored close protection and movement planning. Winstar's draft does not import BM's executive driving or evasive-driving claims.
- [SGA special services](https://site2.sgasecurity.com/our-services/special-services): VIP protection and escorts as an assignment-based service.
- [Transguard escorting](https://www.transguard.ae/en/020107.aspx?id=2): separates escorting people and valuable shipments. Its airside authorisations do not transfer to Winstar.

No armed personnel, armoured vehicles, guaranteed delivery, cash-in-transit, cargo insurance, international coverage, airside permissions or dedicated passenger transport is promised.
