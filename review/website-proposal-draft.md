# Winstar Security
## Website Redesign Proposal

Winstar’s online presence has not kept pace with its growth, operational capability and standing in the security industry. The redesign will bring the website in line with the business today, through a contemporary design, stronger content and a clearer structure.

The website will make Winstar’s services and sector experience easier to explore, bring its people and credentials into focus, and provide a straightforward path from interest to enquiry.

### Design and presentation

The design will build on Winstar’s established identity, combining navy, red and blue with clean layouts, clear typography and strong operational photography. The presentation will be professional and reassuring, with consistent service icons and a restrained use of brand graphics.

The experience will be designed for mobile and desktop, with intuitive navigation, readable content and prominent contact options.

### Content and structure

Content will be rewritten and reorganised around what prospective clients need to know: the services available, how Winstar delivers them and their relevance to different environments. The central message will be proactive protection through vigilant personnel, integrated security systems and coordinated response.

The proposed structure comprises:

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Home | A clear introduction to Winstar, service highlights, sectors, trust signals and enquiry prompts. |
| About Winstar | Company background, operational presence, people, values and credentials. |
| Services | An overview and dedicated pages for the eight service areas. |
| Sectors We Serve | Relevant security needs, solutions and client experience across the five priority sectors. |
| Contact | Office information, direct contact options and a service enquiry form. |
| Careers | A separate route for recruitment enquiries. |
| Privacy | Information on how website enquiry data is handled. |

The service pages will cover Manned Guarding, Alarm Monitoring & Response, Canine Security, VIP & Cargo Escorts, Aviation Security, Electronic Security, Electric Fencing and Facility Management. CCTV and access control will be clearly highlighted within Electronic Security.

### Sectors

A homepage section will introduce the sectors and lead to a dedicated Sectors We Serve page. Each sector will connect its practical security needs with relevant Winstar services and client experience:

- **Real estate and residential developments:** controlled access, property protection and reassurance for residents.
- **Banking and financial services:** vigilant personnel, secure access and professional customer interaction.
- **Aviation:** passenger safety, screening support and controlled movement through sensitive areas.
- **Warehousing and logistics:** protection of goods, premises, vehicle movements and cargo in transit.
- **Hospitality and recreation:** guest safety and a welcoming, professional security presence.

Visitors will be able to move directly from their sector to the relevant services or make an enquiry about their requirements.

### Building confidence

Trust signals will be integrated throughout the design and content, close to the services and claims they support. These will include:

- Relevant client logos and concise examples of work delivered.
- Current industry registration and membership credentials.
- Photography of Winstar’s personnel, equipment and operations.
- Clear explanations of training, supervision, control-room support and incident reporting.
- Company experience, operational presence and accessible office contacts.

This will give visitors a clear sense of both Winstar’s capability and the organisation behind the services.

### Search and AI visibility

The redesign will include foundational SEO and preparation for AI-powered search. Service and sector content will address the questions clients ask, with clear information about Winstar’s capabilities, locations and experience.

This includes page titles and descriptions, internal linking, relevant business structured data, mobile and speed optimisation, crawlable content, an XML sitemap and redirects for replaced pages. Business details will be aligned with the existing Google Business Profile, and Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools will be connected using available account access. Search-crawler access will be checked to support discovery by search engines and AI search services.

These launch foundations are included in the project fee. Search and enquiry tracking will provide a baseline for future improvement; ongoing SEO campaigns and reporting are separate. Rankings and AI citations cannot be guaranteed.

### Functionality and delivery

The website will include an easy-to-manage content system, service enquiry forms with spam protection, click-to-call links, a downloadable company profile and basic enquiry analytics. Search visibility, page speed and accessibility will be addressed as part of the build. Existing website content and links will be reviewed, with redirects for replaced pages where needed.

Delivery will cover content refinement and page planning, design, development, testing and launch, followed by a brief content-management handover. The programme is **two weeks from commencement**, with a design and working-preview review at the end of Week 1 and final review, testing and launch in Week 2.

The project fee is **KES 65,000**, payable as a **KES 20,000 deposit**, **KES 25,000 at the Week 1 milestone** and **KES 20,000 on completion, before launch**. The accompanying quotation sets out the milestones and delivery arrangements. Hosting, domain renewals, paid subscriptions and ongoing maintenance are separate.
