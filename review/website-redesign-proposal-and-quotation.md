# Winstar Security
## Website Redesign Proposal & Quotation

**Project fee: KES 65,000 · Delivery: Two weeks from commencement**

Winstar’s online presence has not kept pace with its growth, operational capability and standing in the security industry. The redesign will bring the website in line with the business today, through a contemporary design, stronger content and a clearer structure.

The website will make Winstar’s services and sector experience easier to explore, bring its people and credentials into focus, and provide a straightforward path from interest to enquiry.

### Design and content

The design will build on Winstar’s established identity, combining navy, red and blue with clean layouts, clear typography and strong operational photography. It will provide a professional, reassuring experience across mobile and desktop, with intuitive navigation and prominent contact options.

Leading industry websites—including Securex Africa, G4S Kenya, BM Security and GardaWorld—will serve as benchmarks. The aim is to exceed them in clarity, ease of use and the strength of Winstar’s own story, while giving the site a distinct identity.

Content will be refined around the services available, how Winstar delivers them and their relevance to different environments. The central message will be proactive protection through vigilant personnel, integrated security systems and coordinated response.

### Website structure

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Home | Introduction, service highlights, sectors, trust signals and enquiry prompts. |
| About Winstar | Company background, operational presence, people, values and credentials. |
| Services | An overview and dedicated pages for the eight service areas. |
| Sectors We Serve | Sector-specific needs, relevant solutions and client experience. |
| Corporate Responsibility & Policies | CSR activities and dedicated Data Protection, Environmental Protection and Anti-Corruption policy pages. |
| Contact | Office information, direct contact options and a service enquiry form. |
| Careers | A separate route for recruitment enquiries. |
| Website Privacy Notice | Clear information on how website enquiries and analytics data are handled, linked from forms and the footer. |

Service pages will cover Manned Guarding, Alarm Monitoring & Response, Canine Security, VIP & Cargo Escorts, Aviation Security, Electronic Security, Electric Fencing and Facility Management. CCTV and access control will be clearly highlighted within Electronic Security.

### Sectors and trust signals

A homepage section will introduce the five priority sectors and lead to a dedicated Sectors We Serve page:

- **Real estate and residential developments:** controlled access, property protection and reassurance for residents.
- **Banking and financial services:** vigilant personnel, secure access and professional customer interaction.
- **Aviation:** passenger safety, screening support and controlled movement.
- **Warehousing and logistics:** protection of goods, premises, vehicle movements and cargo in transit.
- **Hospitality and recreation:** guest safety and a welcoming security presence.

Each sector will connect to relevant services and an enquiry route. Trust signals will appear throughout the site: client logos and concise project examples, current registration and membership credentials, operational photography, and clear explanations of training, supervision, control-room support and incident reporting.

### Corporate responsibility and policies

A dedicated section will present Winstar’s corporate social responsibility activities and commitments, supported by relevant stories and photography. It will also provide clear access to the **Data Protection Policy**, **Environmental Protection Policy** and **Anti-Corruption Policy** through individual pages and downloadable documents where available.

This content will strengthen the company’s governance and trust credentials and make supporting information easy for clients and procurement teams to find. Policy links will remain accessible in the footer, with a separate website privacy notice explaining enquiry and analytics data handling. The scope includes editing, formatting and publishing company-approved policy content.

### SEO and Generative Engine Optimization


The redesign includes foundational **Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)** and **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** to support visibility in traditional search and AI-generated answers. Clear service descriptions, useful answers to client questions and consistent business information will make Winstar’s capabilities easier to discover, understand and reference.

The work includes page titles and descriptions, internal linking, relevant business structured data, crawlable content, an XML sitemap, redirects for replaced pages, and mobile and speed optimisation. Business details will be aligned with the existing Google Business Profile. Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools will be connected using available account access, and search-crawler access will be checked.

These are launch foundations; rankings and inclusion in AI-generated answers cannot be guaranteed.

### Functionality and delivery

The website will include an easy-to-manage content system, enquiry forms with spam protection, click-to-call links, a downloadable company profile and basic enquiry analytics. Delivery includes accessibility checks, testing, launch and a brief content-management handover.
