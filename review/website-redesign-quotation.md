# Winstar Security
## Website Redesign Quotation

**Date:** 24 September 2026  
**Project fee:** KES 65,000  
**Delivery period:** Two weeks from commencement

The quotation covers the website redesign outlined in the accompanying proposal: refreshed design, content refinement and restructuring, service and sector pages, integrated trust signals, and a responsive website for mobile and desktop.

Included are content management, enquiry forms, click-to-call links, a downloadable company profile, basic search optimisation and analytics, testing, launch and handover.

**SEO and AI search visibility are included:** service and sector content optimisation, page titles and descriptions, internal linking, relevant business structured data, crawlability checks, sitemap, redirects, mobile and speed optimisation, and search monitoring setup through Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools using available account access. Business details will be aligned with the existing Google Business Profile. This covers launch foundations; ongoing SEO campaigns and reporting are separate. Rankings and AI citations are not guaranteed.

Work begins upon receipt of the deposit and access to the existing website and hosting. The two-week programme allows for review at the end of Week 1 and final review during Week 2; timely consolidated feedback will keep delivery on schedule.
